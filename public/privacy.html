Privacy Policy

UK Life Simulator is a free browser game. This page explains what happens to your information.

What we store

The game saves a random player ID and your best score in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device except for purchase checks.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Paystack. We receive the email you type and a payment reference. We never see or store your card details.

Ads

The game may show ads from Google AdSense or sponsor messages. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
